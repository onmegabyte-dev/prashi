## 1. Signing in and out
1. Go to `your-website/login`.
2. Type your **email** and **password**, click **Sign in**.
3. When you finish, click **Sign out** (top right). Do this on shared computers.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email. A link arrives (valid 1 hour). If no email arrives, ask another admin to use **Settings → Staff logins → Reset password**.

**Change your own password:** left menu → **Account → Change my password**.

Five wrong passwords lock that login for 15 minutes.

## 2. The dashboard
Shows **New enquiries**, **New registrations**, **Active students**, **Upcoming batches** and **Open doubts**. Click any box to open the full list. Below you will see the latest enquiries, upcoming batches and recent activity.

## 3. Change the website

Everything under **Website content** works the same way:

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Add** something | Click **+ Add …** (top right of the list). Fill the form. Click **Save as draft** (hidden) or **Save & publish** (visible straight away). |
| **Edit** | Click the name or **Edit**, change it, click **Save changes**. |
| **Preview before publishing** | On **Courses** and **Articles** click **Preview**. It opens a new tab showing the page exactly as visitors will see it – even while hidden. |
| **Show / hide** | Click **Show** / **Hide** (testimonials and results say **Approve** / **Unapprove**; articles say **Publish** / **Unpublish**). Hidden things are not deleted – you can bring them back. |
| **Change the order** | Click **▲** or **▼** (works when the list is not sorted – click **Clear** if needed). |
| **Delete** | Click **Delete** and confirm. This cannot be undone – prefer **Hide**. |
| **Find something** | Use the search box, the filters above the list, and click a column heading to sort. |

Fields marked with a **?** show a plain-English tip when you hover or tap them.

### 3.1 Courses
*Website content → Courses → + Add course*
* **Course name, class level, exam, type, duration, subjects, key points, schedule** appear on the course card and page.
* **Subjects:** separate with commas (`Physics, Chemistry`). **Key points:** one per line.
* **Fee (₹)** is **private by default**. Visitors see “Contact us”. Tick **Show fee on website** only if you want that course’s fee public.
* **Number of instalments**, **Lead teacher** and **Course picture** are optional. **Full description** uses the editor (bold, lists, links, pictures).
* A course that still has **batches** cannot be deleted – delete or move the batches first, or just **Hide** the course.

### 3.2 Batches
*Website content → Batches → + Add batch*
1. Choose the **Course**, give the batch a **name**, **start date**, **timing**, **total seats**.
2. **Status:** *Open* (taking admissions), *Full* (shows “join waiting list”), *Closed* (disappears from the website).
3. Seats left are calculated from the students you enrol. A batch with enrolled students cannot be deleted – set it to *Closed* instead.

### 3.3 Faculty
*Website content → Faculty → + Add teacher.* Upload a clear head-and-shoulders photo (JPG/PNG/WebP, up to 5 MB). Without a photo the teacher’s initials are shown.

### 3.4 Testimonials and Results
* **Testimonials** appear only after you click **Approve**. Get the family’s permission before using a name or photo.
* **Results** has three kinds: **Rank holder** (name, exam, rank), **Year success rate** (year + %), **Score improvement** (label + %). Only approved results appear.

### 3.5 FAQs and Notices
* **FAQs:** type the question and answer (the editor supports bold/lists). Choose a course to show it only on that course’s page. **Publish on** / **Expires on** schedule it (Indian time).
* **Notices:** choose **Who should see it?**
  * *Public website* → appears on the home page.
  * *Students only* → appears only in the student portal (optionally for **one batch**).
  * **Pin** keeps it on top. Use **Publish on** and **Expires on** to schedule.

### 3.6 Articles (Resources page)
*Website content → Articles → + Add article.* Write in the editor; the picture button uploads an image into the text. Click **Save as draft**, then **Preview**, then **Publish** on the list. Set **Publish on** to a future date to schedule it.

### 3.7 Holidays
*Website content → Holidays.* Add closure dates; upcoming ones are listed on the Contact page and past ones disappear by themselves.

## 4. Contact details, banner, map, WhatsApp
*Settings → Site settings.* Change and click **Save settings** – the site updates immediately.
* **Announcement bar** – the coloured strip at the top of every page. Empty = hidden.
* **WhatsApp number** – digits with country code (`919876543210`; a 10-digit Indian number gets 91 added). Empty = no WhatsApp button.
* **Email for new enquiries** – where alerts are sent (not shown publicly).
* **Google Maps embed link** – in Google Maps click *Share → Embed a map → Copy HTML*, paste it here. Empty = no map.
* **Social links** – full `https://…` address, or empty to hide.
* **Home page numbers** – students taught, success rate, faculty, years. Use true figures.

### 4b. Edit the words on your pages (Page text)
*Settings → Page text* (admin only). This is where you change the **heading and sentence at the top of the home page** (for example “Learn Today. Lead Tomorrow.” and “Prashi Tutorial coaches Classes 6–12…”), and the other wording that appears on the public site.

1. Click a section to open it: **Home page**, **About page**, **Admissions page**, **Footer & receipts**, **Privacy Policy**, **Terms & Conditions**.
2. Edit the boxes. What each one controls is written under its label (hover the **?** too).
3. Click **Save page text** at the bottom. The website updates at once – click **View website ↗** to look.

Good to know:
* **Home heading** has two parts: the first is plain text, the second is shown in the blue-purple gradient. The second part can be left empty.
* **Lists** (“Why families choose us”, “How we teach”, About → Our journey, Admission steps) are one item per line written as **Title | Description** – the vertical bar `|` separates the two. Up to 12 lines. **Empty the box to hide that whole section.**
* **Privacy Policy / Terms** use the same editor as articles. You can type `{{site_name}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{phone}}` or `{{address}}` and they are filled in from Site settings.
* **Reset this section to the original wording** puts back the sample text (asks first).
* **Please read every box once and save.** The sample wording includes claims such as class sizes, CCTV/safety and monthly parent reports. Only keep what is true for Prashi Tutorial. The go-live checker will not pass until you have saved this screen once.

## 5. Change the forms (Form builder)
*Settings → Form builder.* Choose a form tab: **Admission form**, **Enquiry form**, **Counselling request**, **Contact form**.

**Add a question** (example: “Previous school”, required)
1. Scroll to **Add a question to the … form**.
2. **Question:** `Previous school`. **Answer type:** Short text. Tick **Required**. Click **Add question**.
3. Check the **Preview** panel on the right – that is what visitors see. It is live on the website immediately.

Other actions on each row: **Edit** (rename, change type, hint), **Make required / optional**, **Hide / Show**, **▲ ▼** (order), **Delete**.
* For a **Drop-down**, type one choice per line.
* **Core fields** (name, phone…) are marked *core*: you can rename them but not delete or hide them, so every enquiry has a way to reach the family.
* Hiding or deleting a question never deletes answers people already gave.
* Every form also has a **consent tick-box** for the Privacy Policy that visitors must tick.

## 6. Handle enquiries and admissions
Every form submitted on the website lands in **People → Enquiries & admissions** (and an email goes to your enquiry address).

1. **Find** things with the search box (name, phone, any answer) and filters: *form*, *status*, *assigned to*.
2. Click a **name** to open it. You see every answer.
3. Under **Follow-up**:
   * **Status:** *New → Contacted → Demo booked → Enrolled* (or *Closed*). Update it after each call.
   * **Assigned to:** choose the counsellor responsible.
4. **Private notes:** write what happened (“Called, coming Saturday”). Only staff see notes – never visitors.
5. For an admission that is going ahead click **Create student account** – their details are copied into the new-student form (see next section).
6. **⬇ Download CSV** on the list saves exactly what you are looking at as a spreadsheet (opens in Excel). Use filters first to export just one group.

Only admins can delete an enquiry.

## 7. Add a student, enrol and record fees
*People → Students → + Add student*
1. Fill the details. A **parent phone or parent email is required** so you can always reach the family.
2. Optionally choose a **batch** – the student is enrolled and the fee is taken from the course.
3. Click **Create student and login**. The next screen shows the **login name** (their admission number, like `pt260001`) and a **temporary password – shown only once**. Click **Print this** or write it down and give it to the family. The student must choose their own password at first sign-in.

**Open a student** (click their name) to:
* **Assign to a batch** → choose the batch, optionally type a different **Fee** or a **Discount**, click **Enrol**.
* **Record fees paid** → in the batch box type **Amount**, **Date**, **Mode**, click **Record payment**. A receipt number is created automatically. The **balance updates by itself**. You cannot record more than the balance.
* **Change fee, discount or status** (*active / completed / dropped*) → open the small “Change fee…” section. *Dropped* keeps the history but removes the batch from the balance and from the student’s material access.
* **Profile picture:** students upload and change their own picture from **My profile** in their portal (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP up to 5 MB). You and your staff see it on the student’s page. It is private – never shown on the public website.
* **Print a receipt** → in the payments table click the receipt number (🖨). A clean receipt opens in a new tab with your name and address, the amount in figures **and words**, the mode, and the balance after that payment. Click **Print receipt**. Students can print their own receipts from their portal. Registration/GST line and the note at the bottom are set in *Page text → Footer & receipts*.
* **Edit details**, **Reset password** (new temporary password; the student is signed out everywhere), **Switch off login** (blocks access instantly, deletes nothing).
* **Delete student** (admin only) removes the student and **all** their fees, attendance and records permanently. Prefer *Switch off login*.

Lists: search by name/admission number/parent, filter by **class**, **batch**, or **Fees due**, and sort by balance.

### Import many students from a spreadsheet
*People → Students → ⬆ Import from spreadsheet.* Use this to enter students you already have.
1. Click **Download template (CSV)** and open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Fill **one student per row** (columns are explained on the screen). Only **name** and a **parent phone or parent email** are required. To enrol a student, write the **batch** name exactly as in the admin; fees default to the course fee, and **fees_paid_so_far** records money already received before today.
2. Save as **CSV UTF-8** (Excel: File → Save As). Up to **200 students** per file; split bigger lists.
3. Choose the file and click **Upload and check**. You get a **preview** – **nothing is created yet**. Each row shows ✔ ready or ✘ with the reason (missing contact, unknown batch, bad date, duplicate of an existing student…).
4. Click **Import N students**. Rows with problems are skipped (fix them in the sheet and import just those later).
5. The next page lists every new student’s **login name and temporary password – shown only once.** Click **Print this list** or **Download as CSV** *before* leaving the page. Each student must choose their own password at first sign-in.

Importing the same file twice is safe – students who already exist are detected and skipped.

## 8. Share study material, test results and remarks
*People → Tutorial records → + Add record.*

1. **What is it?** Study material, Notes, Test paper, Mock test result or Remark.
2. **Who is it for?** *One student* or *A whole batch* (class notes can go to everyone in a batch).
3. Give it a **Title**, optional **Text**, optional **file** (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text, ZIP, JPG, PNG up to 20 MB). For a **mock test result** also enter the **Score** and **Out of**.
4. **Visible to student** – leave it **unticked** and the record stays hidden. Students see only records you have made visible.

**Release many at once**
* Tick the records in the list → **Release selected to students** (or **Hide selected**).
* Or scroll down to **Release a whole batch at once**: choose a batch (and optionally a type) → **Release**. That makes every hidden record for that batch and its current students visible.

**Hide / Release** buttons on each row switch a single record. Files are private: only the right students can download them, through their portal.

## 9. Attendance
*People → Attendance.*
1. Choose the **batch** and the **date**, click **Show**.
2. Everyone starts as **Present**. Change only the exceptions (*Absent, Late, Excused*) and add a remark if useful.
3. Click **Save attendance**. You can reopen the same day and correct it.

The “Last 30 days” column shows each student’s attendance. Students see their own in their portal.

## 10. Student doubts
*People → Student doubts.* Questions from students appear with the newest open ones first. Type an answer and click **Send reply** – the student sees it in their portal. **Close without reply** tidies up a question that no longer needs an answer.

## 11. Staff logins
*Settings → Staff logins* (admin only).
* **Add a staff login:** name, email, role.
  * **Staff** – enquiries, students, tutorial records, attendance, doubts. They cannot change website content, settings, other logins, the activity log or downloads, and cannot delete students.
  * **Admin** – everything.
* A **temporary password** is shown once; the person must choose their own at first sign-in.
* **Reset password** and **Switch off** are on each row. Switching someone off signs them out immediately. You cannot switch off your own login.
* Keep **at least two admins** so you can never be locked out.

## Question bank (admin and teachers)
**People → Question bank** stores questions for the future quiz mode.
1. Click **+ Upload questions**, then choose the **batch, class (6–12 or Custom), subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Custom)**, type the **chapter**, and choose the **question type** (1–5 marks or Custom).
2. Upload a **PDF, Word (.docx) or text (.txt)** file. Number the questions (`1.` `2.` `3.` or `Q1.` `Q2.`), or put each on its own line. Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) cannot be read.
3. Check the questions it found, fix the wording or untick any, then **Save**. Questions already in the bank are skipped.
Every question gets a **Q-ID** that counts up inside its class + subject + chapter + type, and is never reused. Questions are kept even if the teacher who added them, or the batch, is removed later. Only the admin can delete a question.

## 12. Activity log, downloads and backups
* **Settings → Activity log** (admin) shows who changed what and when: sign-ins, edits, deletions, payments, downloads. Filter by person, area, kind of change or date. Entries cannot be edited. To keep the log short, an admin can clear old entries (older than 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or everything) with the **Clear** button at the bottom of the page; download the log first if you need it. The clearing itself is always recorded.
* **Settings → Download data**: one Excel file per section of your data (23 files), or everything in one ZIP. Created fresh when you click. Passwords are never included. These files contain personal data – store and share them carefully.
* **Backups** are made with `python scripts/backup.py` on the server (see the README). Download an Excel/ZIP copy now and then as well.

## 13. When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| “This page was open too long… Nothing was saved” | Refresh the page and try again. |
| “Access denied” | Your login doesn’t have permission for that screen. Ask an admin. |
| A form says a field is required / invalid | Fix the red message under the field and save again. |
| “…cannot be deleted because it is still in use” | Something depends on it (a course with batches, a batch with students). Hide it instead, or remove what depends on it first. |
| I deleted something by mistake | Deleted items cannot be restored from the admin. Ask your developer to restore the latest backup, or re-create it. |
| Something is on the website that shouldn’t be | Click **Hide** on that item – it vanishes immediately. |
| A student can’t sign in | Check their login is not switched off, then **Reset password** and give them the new temporary password. |
